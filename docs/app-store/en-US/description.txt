Plan your race with Running Race Mate. Keep your goal time, pace, fueling, gear, and notes together so you can review your plan before race day.

Create a plan for each race, check your target pace and split times, and use a checklist and reminders to prepare. Add a pre-race schedule for registration, travel, baggage drop, and other activities.

Running Race Mate Pro is a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks detailed checkpoints and cutoffs, pace strategies and A/B/C plans, fueling and hydration plans, advanced checklists, reusable plans, and Race Card exports as images, PDFs, or printable pace bands. Your saved races and basic planning features remain available for free. The price is shown in the app before purchase.

Your race plans and notes are stored on your device. No account is required. The app does not provide medical, nutrition, or training advice; make your own decisions and consult a qualified professional when needed.
